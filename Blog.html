Marina Beach

Marina Beach is the longest urban beach in India. Located right in the heart of Chennai, it is surrounded by historical monuments, ancient temples, and museums. It is highly convenient to combine a beach trip with several iconic cultural landmarks.

Mahabalipuram Trip

Mahabalipuram (Mamallapuram) is a historic UNESCO World Heritage site in Tamil Nadu, famed for 7th- and 8th-century Pallava dynasty rock-cut architecture. It features stunning ancient temples, giant open-air rock reliefs, pristine beaches, and natural wonders

Mumbai Gate

Standing tall as a symbol of resilience and heritage, the Gateway of India is more than just yellow basalt stone and intricate archways. Built to welcome royalty in 1911, it has witnessed the shifting tides of time, standing as a proud sentinel where the Arabian Sea meets the bustling energy of the "City of Dreams"
